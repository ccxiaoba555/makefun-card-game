**Ember Sigil Duel** — a turn-based card battler. You are the Ashen Warden, duelling the Cinder Witch across a felt table: play cards from your hand (each costs mana, deals its ATK as damage, gains its SHD as shield and heals its HEAL), end your turn and watch the witch answer. Mana refills and grows every round. Reduce her to 0 HP to win; if your own life reaches 0, the duel is lost. 24 original cards in four elements — Ember, Leaf, Tide and Stone — with synthesized sound effects and keyboard play (1–5 to play a card, E to end turn, R to restart).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests, no dependencies.

The screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of the game: the duel table (cover), the title screen, victory and defeat. `?shot=title|play|win|loss&seed=1` stages each scene deterministically.
